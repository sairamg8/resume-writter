# Parity table: Design panel, template picker, layout and look controls (area `editor-design-templates`)

Audit of the live source against the canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/SjfCTE1dSTgt1UY63uoFiM, version 1791271695-af84) under `PARITY-RULE.md`: the redesign changes the UI only. Read-only audit; no test was run, nothing was built.

**Boards that draw this area:** ET (the gallery modal over the editor), MD (phone Design sheet), and the Design drawer on ED, EP and ET (also behind the modals on EditorImprove, EditorShare and EditorDownload) with the rail Template / Color / Type / Spacing / Headings / More; only the **Color** pane is drawn, with Accent swatches, "Accent is used on" toggles, Text chips, `Reset design`, `Changes save as you go`. ES, EL, EditorAts and EditorDraft draw the Design button in the stage toolbar but NO drawer (corrected by review: the audit said ES and EL draw it). MD draws no Reset design and no footer.

**Counts** (88 audit rows plus 15 added by review, see section 1B: 103 rows): SAME 18, MOVED 7, RESTYLED 12, CHANGED 7, MISSING 59. (The audit's own tally of its 88 rows was SAME 11, MOVED 6, RESTYLED 12, CHANGED 7, MISSING 52 and is correct.)

**Headlines for the lead**
1. The drawer draws only the Color pane. Everything in Template, Type, Spacing, Headings and More (about 60 functions: Save my design, 13 fonts, Add a Google Font, 1-Page Fit, Page size, margins, heading styles, Date format, bullets, links, page numbers, contact icon packs, Sidebar layout) has a rail slot or none and no drawing: these are MISSING rows, the fix is to draw the panes, never to drop the functions.
2. Four drawn behaviours contradict the live app and need a canvas change: the gallery's Cancel / Use template (live: apply on click, Undo toast, Done), the 7-swatch accent palette (live: 8 other hexes), the 3 text chips (live: 4 + custom), and "Accent is used on" toggles in place of the Name / Job title colour pickers.
3. Not drawn at all and easy to lose: the view-mode toggle (Editor only / Split / Preview only), the panel resize handle, the font-fallback notice, the per-group reset arrows with their Undo toasts, the Reset confirm, the `?tab=design` deep link, and the phone gallery sheet and More chip.
4. The 8 template names on ET are the live designs (Harbor ... Inkwell), not placeholders; the other 20 cards (19 templates + the Sidebar single-column card) must also be in the gallery.

Files (all under `src/`): DesignPanel.jsx, DesignPanelShared.jsx, DesignPanelTemplate.jsx, DesignPanelColors.jsx, DesignPanelTypography.jsx, DesignPanelHeadings.jsx,
DesignPanelDates.jsx, DesignPanelLists.jsx, DesignPanelLinks.jsx, DesignPanelPageNumbers.jsx, DesignPanelLayout.jsx, TemplateGallery.jsx, TemplateThumb.jsx, LayoutToggle.jsx,
FontFallbackNotice.jsx, EditorHeader.jsx, EditorPreviewPane.jsx are in `components/`; Editor.jsx is in `pages/`; useResumeStore.js, useResumeDesignActions.js, usePickCard.js,
usePanelResize.js, useEditorTab.js, useTypedNumber.js are in `hooks/`; templatePicker.js, templateSwitch.js, defaultData.js, fonts.js, pageFit.js, publicLink.js are in `utils/`;
templates.js, templateTable.js, templatePresets.js, layoutOptions.js, designNumbers.js, spacingNumbers.js, pageSize.js, skillLevels.js are in `constants/`.
Bare `Name.jsx:N` means the file of that name above. Boards (canvas files `project/<Name>.dc.html`): ED = Editor, ET = EditorTemplates, EP = EditorPersonal, ES = EditorSection,
EL = EditorLetter, MD = MobileDesign, ST = States. Line numbers are the worktree's source on 2026-10-06.
